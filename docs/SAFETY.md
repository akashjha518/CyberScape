# Safety and privacy guidelines

CyberEscape teaches defensive concepts through ordinary, public environmental observations. It is not a real-world security audit.

- Observe only ordinary, publicly visible features.
- Do not identify, photograph, follow, profile, or collect information about people.
- Do not read or record personal or confidential information.
- Do not test networks, devices, locks, access controls, or security equipment.
- Do not trespass, touch or move objects, or interact with strangers.
- Do not seek blind spots, targets, or exploitable weaknesses.
- Do not claim a location is vulnerable based on casual observation.
- Put the phone away during the walk and prioritize personal safety.

If no suitable feature is available, choose another ordinary observation or reflect on the general defensive principle.
